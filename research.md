# Research: Water Camper Tracking

**The Problem**
Security guards at hostels and apartments struggle to track 20-liter drinking water campers. Suppliers drop off full campers in the morning and pick up empty ones later. Students take and return campers randomly throughout the day. Tracking this manually on paper leads to missed entries and arguments over lost bottles. A simple tool is needed to calculate the current inventory automatically.

**Existing Solutions:**

1. Paper Registers: Guards write it down manually, but pages get lost and calculating daily totals takes too much time.

2. WhatsApp Messages: They text the supplier, but this only records orders, not the live count of bottles inside the building.
