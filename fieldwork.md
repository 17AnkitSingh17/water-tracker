# Fieldwork Notes

* **Location:** Main Entrance Gate of NIT Jamshedpur Hostel L, Jamshedpur , Jharkhand, India
* **Person:** Sashikant Mahto (Security Guard)
* **3 Things he Said:**
    1. "It is very hard to write down every time a student takes a bottle because I am busy managing the gate traffic."
    2. "Suppliers sometimes claim they delivered 50 bottles, but they actually only drop off 48."
    3. "Students often forget to return the empty bottles, which causes a shortage the next morning."

* **1 Thing That Surprised Me:** I thought the guard counted the bottles only at night, but he actually has to count them 3 to 4 times a day to make sure none are stolen.

* **How I Collected Data:** I sat with the guard for 20 minutes, looked at his rough paper register, and copied 20 real entries from today into my CSV file.
